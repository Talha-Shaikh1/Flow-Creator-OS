### 2. Strict Multi-Character Speaker Isolation (Zero Dialogue Mix-Ups)

The biggest flaw in multi-character AI video generation is mouth misattribution. FlowCreator OS solves this with a two-step spatial directing framework:

1. **Master Frame Two/Three-Shot Setup (Step 1):**
   * The start-frame image is mathematically partitioned: **Hero on Screen-Left**, **Villain on Screen-Right**, and **Supporting Ally in Center/Background**, each with locked visual anchors.
2. **Explicit Lip-Sync Command Isolation (Step 2):**
   * In every 10-second scene prompt, FlowCreator OS explicitly directs Google Flow:
     > *"Over-the-shoulder push-in on LEO (Screen-Left), only his animated mouth articulating the dialogue. In background on screen-right, MARCUS stays completely silent, glaring with a clenched jaw."*
   * This guarantees that when the camera cuts, only the designated character speaks while the other reacts realistically in silence.

### 3. Hollywood Micro-Directing & Eye-Contact Psychology

Generic AI videos feel robotic because characters stare blankly. FlowCreator OS injects granular micro-directing into every single 10-second scene:
